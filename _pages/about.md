I am a fifth year PhD student in the [Operations Research and Financial Engineering (ORFE)](https://orfe.princeton.edu/) department at Princeton University, co-advised by Prof. [Amir Ali Ahmadi](https://aaa.princeton.edu/) and Prof. [Bartolomeo Stellato](https://stellato.io/). 

My research focuses on mathematical optimization and its applications in machine learning, combinatorial problems, and beyond. In particular, my work has brought a convex perspective to neural network training and developed new computational frameworks for polynomial optimization. For example, my *Disjunctive Sum of Squares* paper proposes a new hierarchy for certifying nonnegativity of polynomials with multiple low-degree algebraic identities.

I received my undergraduate degree from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Peking University.
